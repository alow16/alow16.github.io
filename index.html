I'm a student at Stanford studying electrical engineering and computer science. Before moving to the Bay, I grew up in Orange County, California.

Currently, I'm a fellow at Comma Capital, an early-stage fund based in NYC. Previously, I worked on nanomaterials for thermal transport in high-performance computing and the formability of magnesium alloys.

In an earlier life, I was named a Regeneron Science Talent Search Scholar, competed in the U.S. National Chemistry Olympiad, and won the VEX World Championship.

At the moment, I'm particularly interested in the frontier of scientific superintelligence and generalist robotics.
